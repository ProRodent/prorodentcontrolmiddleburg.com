How a visit works

The same four steps apply whether the call is about a mouse in the kitchen or rats in the attic.

  1. Inspect

    A technician checks the attic, garage and exterior to find activity and every entry point.

  2. Treat

    Traps and stations go where activity is heaviest, matched to mice or rats.

  3. Seal

    Gaps, vents and gnawed openings are closed with materials rodents cannot chew through.

  4. Follow up

    We return to check the traps, confirm the seals hold and advise on cleanup.

Signs you have rodents

Check your home for these. Two or more usually means an active problem.

(888) 686-4821

  • Droppings

    Dark pellets along baseboards, in drawers, under sinks or in the attic.

  • Gnaw marks

    Fresh gnawing leaves pale, rough wood on frames, wiring and packaging.

  • Noises at night

    Scratching or scurrying in walls, ceilings or under floors after dark.

  • Nesting material

    Shredded paper, insulation or fabric packed into hidden corners.

  • Rub marks

    Greasy smears along walls, fences and pipes on regular routes.

  • Odors

    A musty, ammonia-like smell in closed rooms or near walls.

Questions about rodent control

How can I tell if I have mice or rats?

Look for droppings, gnaw marks, greasy rub marks and night-time scratching in walls or the attic. Rat droppings are noticeably larger than mouse droppings.

How do rodents get into Middleburg homes?

Mice can pass through gaps of about a quarter inch and rats through roughly half an inch, so pipes, vents, garage door gaps and roof lines near tree cover are common entry points.

Will traps alone solve the problem?

Trapping removes the animals inside, but sealing the entry points is what stops new ones from arriving.

Is treatment safe around children and pets?

Tell the technician about every child and pet in the home, and we explain what was placed and where before we leave.

How quickly can someone come out?

Our line is answered around the clock and same-day visits are offered where the schedule allows.

What happens after the first visit?

We check the traps, confirm the seals are holding and advise on cleanup.
