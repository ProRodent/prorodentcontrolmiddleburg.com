What rodents damage most.

Chewed wiring, gnawed ductwork, damaged insulation, and holes in drywall or soffits are the most common repair calls after a rodent problem in Middleburg. Wiring damage is the most serious, since it can create a fire risk and is not always visible without opening a wall or attic access.

Older homes near the center of town sometimes have original wiring that is more vulnerable to chew damage than newer romex, so a rodent problem in an older house is more likely to turn into an electrical repair as well.

What a repair visit covers.

The technician documents damage with photos before repair work starts, which is useful if you need it for insurance or for a landlord. Repairs are scheduled after trapping and sealing are complete, so new damage does not happen before the old damage is fixed.

  • Photos taken of all damage before repair begins.
  • Insulation, ductwork, and drywall repaired or replaced as needed.
  • Wiring repairs coordinated with a licensed electrician where required.
Repair without sealing just gives rodents something new to chew.

Ductwork and insulation.

Chewed ductwork loses efficiency and can pull contaminated attic air into the house, so damaged sections are patched or replaced rather than left as-is. Insulation that has been shredded for nesting is removed and replaced to restore its performance.

After repairs.

Do not touch damaged wiring yourself, and have photos ready when you call if you have already noticed chew marks or exposed wire. Repairs are always paired with a check that the original entry point has been sealed, so the same damage does not happen again.

Homes with attic access near tree cover, common throughout older parts of Middleburg, tend to need more extensive insulation replacement than newer construction, simply because established rodent routes through mature trees create repeat entry over time if sealing is delayed. Repair scope is always confirmed with photos and a written estimate before work begins, so there are no surprises once the job is underway.

Before we arrive

  • Keep children and pets away from any area with droppings, a nest, or a suspected entry point.
  • Do not sweep or vacuum dry droppings; that can spread contamination into the air.
  • Do not set out your own loose bait or poison before the visit.
  • Note when and where you have seen or heard activity so the technician can start there.

Questions about rodent damage repair

Do you repair drywall as well as wiring?

Yes, drywall, insulation, ductwork, and coordination with an electrician for wiring are all part of repair work.

How soon after removal should repairs happen?

As soon as sealing is complete, since leaving damage unaddressed can invite the same problem again.

Do I need an electrician for wiring damage?

For any chewed wiring, yes. We coordinate with a licensed electrician where wiring repair is needed.

What kind of damage do you repair?

Chewed wiring coordination, insulation, ductwork and drywall are the most common repairs after a rodent problem.

Is repair scheduled separately from removal?

Repair typically happens after removal and sealing are complete, so nothing gets damaged again during the repair itself.

Repair work across Middleburg.

Repair visits cover homes across Middleburg, from older sections near the creek to newer construction further out along the highway. Have photos of any chewed wiring or insulation ready when you call, and do not touch damaged wiring yourself.
