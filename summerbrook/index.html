Questions about Summerbrook

How do rodents get into Middleburg homes?

Mice can pass through gaps of about a quarter inch and rats through roughly half an inch, so pipes, vents, garage door gaps and roof lines near tree cover are common entry points.

Why is ground-level entry more common in Summerbrook?

Younger landscaping means trees have not grown into roofline contact yet, so garage doors and utility penetrations are the more common entry points.

Does Now & Then Mall affect nearby homes?

Streets closest to the mall occasionally see rodent pressure pushed outward from commercial dumpsters and delivery areas.
